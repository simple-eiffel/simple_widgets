What shipped: the six waves (2026-08-21 → 2026-08-23)

WaveDelivered
1The runtime and the core vocabulary: window, painter, theme, buttons, text, choices, layout.
2Text engine (selection, clipboard, spelling), lists, dialogs, menus, the drawn file dialog.
3Disclosure (tabs, accordion, drawers, popovers), indicators, pickers, culture (SW_LOCALE), data grid, tree, colour picker, the dev-mode suite, 7GUIs complete.

5Enterprise: kanban, gantt, dock zones, spreadsheet, avatar, statistic, timeline; the inspector lens and force mesh matured.
6Media + conversational: carousel, gallery, transport, crop, chat thread, prompt view, and SW_DICTATION (whisper.cpp through the speechkit cluster - real transcription proven in the suite).

THE CARVE (2026-08-23): every Win32 external moved to simple_shell (deferred SHELL_WINDOW - descend and effect dispatch). simple_widgets is now pure Eiffel over two substrates: simple_cairo draws, simple_shell pumps. The dividend: SW_SCREEN - desktop capture as a CAIRO_SURFACE.

The method carried the whole way: every control ships with demo wiring, contract assaults, and its own documentation page; specs/S04-ROADMAP.md is the per-control docket, harvested from the same sources as the pages so the two cannot drift. This page is the narrative view; S04 is the checklist; S02 is the charter.

The deepening sweep (in flight)

The standing directive: go back over the whole library and handle every known limit, bug, gotcha and plan - or explain honestly why not. The ledger is specs/S04-ROADMAP.md (339 items). Every fall is proven by a failing-first or behaviour-pinning assault before it ships.

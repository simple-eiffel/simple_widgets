Description

Declare text/number/check/choice fields and the labelled column builds itself from shipped organs. value_of reads any field as text (checks speak True/False), is_complete reports whether every required field holds something - the model surface assaulted without a pixel. Pairs with Smart Paste when R03 lands, as the roadmap pinned.

Source: src/sw_form_generator.e · since Wave 5 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A form

create fg.make
fg.add_field (class="string">"title", fg.Kind_text, True)
fg.add_choice_field (class="string">"wave", <<class="string">"four", class="string">"five">>, False)
fg.set_value (class="string">"title", class="string">"hello")
fg.is_complete   -- True

Known limits

  • Validation is presence-only; format rules ride the boxes' own validity machinery later.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Choice fields read back the combo TEXT - free typing is legal there by the combo's nature.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Smart Paste filling; per-field validators; two-column layouts.

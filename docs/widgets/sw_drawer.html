SW_DRAWER

Description

The pushpin: every open drawer wears a drawn pin in its header, left of the X - filled accent while pinned, hollow while peeking - and the pin is a toggle: click to unpin (auto-hide re-arms; the drawer closes when the pointer leaves), click to pin again. Unpinning is exempt from the interact-pins-implicitly rule, or it would undo itself.

Overlap law: one overlay at a time - opening any sheet, drawer or popover dismisses the previous, and a centered modal sheet blocks tab peeking entirely. Same-edge drawer overlap is meaningless; opposite-edge coexistence (pinned left + pinned right) is really DOCKING - content reflow - and is planned as such (S04, Wave 5 enterprise composites) rather than faked as stacked overlays.

Tabs, peek and pin (Larry's design, same day): window.add_drawer_tab (label, builder, edge) registers an edge tab living in a reserved GUTTER - a slim rail outside the content and its scrollbars, so aiming at a scrollbar thumb can never open a drawer by accident. Each tab wears a tiny drawn drawer icon over its stacked letters. Hovering the tab peeks the drawer (it closes when the pointer leaves the panel); clicking pins it (stays until dismissed); interacting with a peeked drawer pins it implicitly so it cannot vanish mid-use. Content comes from a builder agent - fresh on every open, the menu freshness law. The edge parameter accepts all four edges so the API never churns; left and right are implemented, top and bottom arrive with S04.

Drawers are an overlay presentation of the window - window.show_drawer (content, width, from_right) slides ANY widget tree in as a full-height edge panel over a lightly dimmed backdrop. Outside clicks, Escape, or your own wiring close it (window.close_sheet closes every overlay kind). The overlay engine is shared: sheets (centered, truly modal), drawers (edge, dismissable), popovers (anchored, light) are one mechanism with four presentations - and popups still open above them all, so drawer widgets keep their menus.

SW_DRAWER itself is the furniture: an SW_COLUMN with a title header and a drawn close X that fires on_close - wire it to window.close_sheet and the X, the backdrop and Escape all agree.

Source: src/sw_drawer.e · since Wave 3 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A settings drawer from a menu

on_open_drawer
    local
        d: SW_DRAWER
    do
        create d.make_titled (class="string">"Settings")
        d.set_on_close (agent on_drawer_close)
        d.put (create {SW_SWITCH}.make (class="string">"Live updates", True, Void))
        d.put (create {SW_LABEL}.make_body (class="string">"Click outside, press Escape, or use the X."))
        window.show_drawer (d, 300.0, True)   -- from the right
    end

on_drawer_close
    do
        window.close_sheet
    end

Known limits

  • No slide animation - the drawer appears (heartbeat easing is queued).
  • One overlay at a time: a drawer displaces an open sheet or popover.
  • Tab gutters: left and right today; Edge_top/Edge_bottom are accepted by the API but precondition-blocked until their gutters land (S04).
  • Peek-close grace SHIPPED 2026-08-23: an unpinned drawer survives one heartbeat outside (about half a second) before closing; re-entry resets the clock.
  • One overlay at a time by law: dual pinned drawers await true docking (Wave 5).

Bugs & gotchas

  • The panel rectangle is tracked so clicking the drawer's own border never dismisses it - but a click in the dimmed area always does. Centered sheets are the modal ones.
  • The X only FIRES on_close - closing is the host's line (window.close_sheet), so a host may intercept (confirm unsaved changes) before closing.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Slide-in/out easing on the heartbeat.
  • Top/bottom edges; pinnable (non-modal docked) mode.
  • Standard footer slot for drawer verbs (Apply/Close).

Description

Wave 4 opened exactly as the roadmap promised: the shared axis/scale engine is the real deliverable; the charts are its faces. SW_SCALE is a pure-math linear map from a data DOMAIN to a pixel RANGE with its inverse (value_at - hover asks what number lives under the pointer), the classic 1/2/5 tick ladder (steps are always 1, 2 or 5 times a power of ten), nice_domain widening to ladder multiples, and honest degenerate behaviour: a flat domain collapses positions to the range midpoint and offers its one tick. Inverted ranges are first-class - a y-axis maps bigger values to smaller pixels.

SW_CHART is the deferred chassis: two scales re-anchored to the plot rectangle every draw (immediate-mode truth), y-gridlines and ladder labels, a push_clip around the data layer so nothing bleeds, a hover layer drawn OUTSIDE the clip so chips may overhang, theme-semantic series colours (accent, success, warning, danger - cycling), and human-width number formatting. SW_PAINTER grew polyline and polygon_fill for the family - R2 holds: the painter grows what charts need; charts never touch cairo.

Descendants implement three things: their data, refresh_domains (public - the assaults interrogate domain math headless), and draw_data inside the clip.

Source: src/sw_chart.e · since Wave 4 opening (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

The engine, alone

create scale.make (0.0, 97.0, 0.0, 500.0)
scale.tick_step (5)      -- 20.0 (the 1/2/5 ladder)
scale.ticks (5)          -- 0, 20, 40, 60, 80
scale.nice_domain (5)    -- domain widens to 0 .. 100
scale.position (50.0)    -- pixel for a value
scale.value_at (260.0)   -- value under a pixel (hover's question)

Known limits

  • Linear only - log/time scales are future movements.
  • One y-axis per chart; twin-axis plots are not planned soon.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Ranges are set by the chassis every draw; setting them yourself only matters in headless math (the assaults do exactly that).
  • nice_domain is one-way widening - re-fitting resets it (refresh_domains does both, every frame).

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Legend machinery shared across the family.
  • Proportions cluster (pie/donut, funnel) - painter arcs exist.
  • Gauges + sparkline (pairs with SW_STATISTIC), then densities and flows.

SW_LINE_CHART

Description

Named series over the chassis, domains auto-fitted (y widened to the ladder). with_area washes under each line at 16% alpha - the roadmap's area chart as an option, not a class. Every series ends in an emphasized endpoint dot. set_capacity makes a series a ROLLING feed: a full series drops its oldest point per arrival, so a live stream stays bounded.

The demo dogfoods the whole stack: its line chart subscribes to the window's after_render_actions bell and streams EVERY FRAME'S RENDER COST live - Wave 4 drinking from the event layer on day one, and an honest perf instrument in the showroom.

Hover snaps a crosshair to the nearest sample of the first series, rings it, and names the pair in a chip clamped inside the frame.

Source: src/sw_line_chart.e · since Wave 4 opening (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A live feed off the render bell

create perf.make
perf.set_capacity (150)
perf.add_series (class="string">"frame ms")
page.put (perf.with_area.with_title (class="string">"live render cost"))

window.after_render_actions.subscribe (agent on_frame_cost)

on_frame_cost (a_ms: INTEGER)
    do
        frame_no := frame_no + 1
        perf.add_point (frame_no.to_double, a_ms.to_double)
    end

Known limits

  • The crosshair follows the FIRST series; per-series hover is future.
  • No legend yet - series are told apart by colour order (accent, success, warning, danger).

Bugs & gotchas

  • add_point appends to the LAST series added - build series fully, in order.
  • A single-point series draws a dot, not a line - honest, occasionally surprising.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Shared legend machinery; per-series hover; step and smoothed interpolation.

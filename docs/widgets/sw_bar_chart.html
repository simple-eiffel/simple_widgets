Description

One series of labelled categories: slots divide the plot evenly, bars sit at 62% of their slot, y runs 0 to the nice ceiling over the max. The x axis writes category labels centred under their slots instead of ladder numbers. Hovering a slot rings its bar and floats the value in a chip. bar_at - the slot arithmetic - is public pure math, assaulted headless.

Source: src/sw_bar_chart.e · since Wave 4 opening (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • Single series today - grouped multi-series bars are a stated future.
  • Values are expected non-negative; the baseline is always zero.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Long category labels can collide underneath narrow slots; abbreviate or widen.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Grouped and stacked series; horizontal orientation.

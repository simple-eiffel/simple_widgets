Description

Raw (x, y) points, both domains fitted and ladder-widened. Dots are accent discs with a surface ring so overlaps stay legible. nearest_point - the same nearest-within-radius idiom the dev mesh uses - answers hover, ringing the dot and naming its pair; it is public, range-anchored math, assaulted headless.

Source: src/sw_scatter_chart.e · since Wave 4 opening (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A deterministic cloud

create sc.make
from i := 1 until i > 48 loop
    sc.add_point (i.to_double, ((i * 37) \\ 97).to_double)
    i := i + 1
end
page.put (sc.with_title (class="string">"48 deterministic points"))

Known limits

  • Uniform dot radius - size-as-third-dimension (bubble) is future.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Dense clouds make hover ambiguous at reach boundaries - the nearest wins.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Bubble radius channel; category colouring per point.

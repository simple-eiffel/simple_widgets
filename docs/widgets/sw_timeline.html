Description

SW_TIMELINE lists events along a vertical rail: a dot per entry wearing its semantic kind (neutral/accent/success/warning/danger), the time right-aligned in mono to the rail's left, the title bold to its right, an optional detail muted beneath. Rows with detail are taller (48px vs 30px) - row_height is public and the assault suite pins the arithmetic.

Source: src/sw_timeline.e · since Wave 3 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

accordion.add_section (class="string">"Timeline", tl)

Known limits

  • Presentational: entries are not clickable and do not scroll internally (host it in a scroll area or accordion for long histories).
  • Details are single-line; long details clip.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Times are strings, not parsed values - ordering is yours; the widget draws what it is given in the order it was given.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Entry click agents (jump to the thing that happened).
  • Grouped day headers; relative-time refresh on the heartbeat.
  • Virtualized backing for thousands of events (SW_LIST marriage).

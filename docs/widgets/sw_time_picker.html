SW_TIME_PICKER

A time field with culture: 12-hour locales speak AM/PM, 24-hour speak HH:MM - generous in, exact out.

Description

SW_TIME_PICKER is the date picker's small sibling: a single-line box whose edits parse per the effective locale's clock. Both notations are always readable - a US-locale field accepts "14:05" and a European one accepts "2:05 PM" (generous in) - but set_time and write-back format strictly per locale (exact out). The 12 AM = hour-zero and 12 PM = noon traps are handled and assault-pinned. Invalid times (25:00, 9:75) wear the tint; on_time_change (hour, minute) fires on good parses with hour always 0..23 regardless of display culture.

Source: src/sw_time_picker.e · since Wave 3 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Both cultures in one row

create time_us.make_picker            -- theme: H:MM AM/PM
create time_eu.make_picker
time_eu := time_eu.with_picker_locale (create {SW_LOCALE}.make_european)
time_us.set_time (14, 5)              -- shows 2:05 PM
time_eu.set_time (14, 5)              -- shows 14:05

Known limits

  • Minutes precision only (no seconds).
  • No spin zones yet - typing only (spinners are queued).
  • Same pre-first-draw locale note as the date picker.

Bugs & gotchas

  • on_time_change always reports 24-hour values - display culture never leaks into the programmatic contract.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Hour/minute spin zones and wheel support.
  • Seconds precision flag; interval snapping (15-minute steps).
  • Pairing with SW_DATE_PICKER into a datetime composite (SIMPLE_DATE_TIME exists).

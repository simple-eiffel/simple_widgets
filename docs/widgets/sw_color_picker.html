Description

SW_COLOR_PICKER draws the classic two-part picker: a saturation/value field banded from the current hue (click or drag to pick), a hue spectrum bar beneath, and a live swatch beside a mono #RRGGBB readout. The model is HSV - hue [0,360), saturation and brightness [0,1], all invariant-clamped - and the border speaks RGB: rgb: NATURAL_32, set_rgb, and on_change delivering 0xRRGGBB on every pick and drag.

The conversions are assault-pinned on the corners: pure red, green, blue, cyan, white, black, and round trips through arbitrary colours. ('value' being a loaded word in agent contexts, the V of HSV is named brightness.)

Source: src/sw_color_picker.e · since Wave 3 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Picking, and being told

create color_picker.make (0x4D8FD6)     -- start on the accent
color_picker.set_on_change (agent on_color_changed)

on_color_changed (a_rgb: NATURAL_32)
    do
        statusbar.set_left ({STRING_32} class="string">"colour: " + color_picker.hex_text)
    end

Known limits

  • No alpha channel (the theme's colours are opaque RGB by design).
  • Hex TYPING shipped 2026-08-23: digits and '#' build in the readout, Enter adopts (#RGB and #RRGGBB), Escape abandons; from_hex is public and assaulted.
  • No palette swatches/recent colours row yet.
  • The banded field renders 288 rects + 36 bar rects per frame - cheap, but a retained-layer future exists if pickers multiply.

Bugs & gotchas

  • set_rgb derives HSV, so a subsequent rgb query may differ by one bit per channel from rounding - compare colours with tolerance, or store what on_change told you.
  • Dragging fires on_change continuously - debounce expensive reactions, never the visual echo.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Hex input - SHIPPED 2026-08-23 (typed straight into the readout; no text-box marriage needed).
  • Palette/recent swatches; eyedropper over the window surface.
  • Alpha variant when a consumer needs translucency.

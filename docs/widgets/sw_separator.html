Description

SW_SEPARATOR draws one horizontal hairline across its width, vertically centered in a 9px band. Use it to score sections apart where a full card would be too heavy.

Source: src/sw_separator.e · since Wave 2 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • Horizontal only (menus and toolbars draw their own vertical gaps).
  • No label-in-line variant ('--- OR ---') yet.

Bugs & gotchas

  • It spans the container's width, not the window's - inside padded columns the line respects the padding, which is almost always what you want.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Vertical variant for rows.
  • Labeled separator (text carved mid-line, SW_GROUP-style).

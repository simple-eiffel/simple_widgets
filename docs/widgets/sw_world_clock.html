Description

The idea's second half: realtime computation of current-time within a timezone. Cities carry MINUTE offsets - India's +5:30 and Nepal's +5:45 are first-class citizens, not rounding errors. The current moment comes from SIMPLE_DATE_TIME.make_now_utc (the ecosystem owns 'now' - no new externals), each row renders through the theme's SW_LOCALE so the 12/24-hour law follows culture, and a +1d/-1d chip tells the truth when a zone lives in tomorrow or yesterday.

The heartbeat's ambient repaint IS the tick - no timers wired anywhere. The zone arithmetic (zone_time, day_delta) is pure public math, assaulted headless across midnight in both directions. Offsets are yours to supply: DST and zone LAW are deliberately not computed here - honesty over false authority.

Source: src/sw_world_clock.e · since Wave 4 coda - Larry's idea, kept (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Ticking cities

create clock.make
clock.add_city (class="string">"Denver (UTC-7)", -420)
clock.add_city (class="string">"Mumbai (UTC+5:30)", 330)
clock.add_city (class="string">"Sydney (UTC+10)", 600)
page.put (clock.with_title (class="string">"world clock"))
-- nothing else: the heartbeat repaint is the tick

Known limits

  • Minutes precision on display (the heartbeat repaints 4x/second; seconds would be theatre at HH:MM anyway).
  • No DST computation - supply winter/summer offsets yourself, or wire a rule table when simple_datetime grows one.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Row times follow the AMBIENT theme locale - a 12-hour theme shows AM/PM everywhere; per-row locale is not a thing (yet).

Extension plans & possibilities

  • A picked-zone row fed live by SW_TIMEZONE_PICKER.on_change; DST rule tables if/when the ecosystem grows them; seconds display behind a flag.

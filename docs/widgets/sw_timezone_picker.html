SW_TIMEZONE_PICKER

The pickable timezone map: click a 15-degree band on the world; on_change fires the UTC offset.

Description

Pinned as an IDEA on day one of the locale work - a timezone set of tools, including a pickable timezone map - and buildable the day SW_MAP landed. It IS the map (inheritance, not wrapping): a click reads its longitude, rounds to the nearest 15-degree band (offset_at, public, assaulted: Greenwich is zero, Denver's meridian is minus seven, the edges clamp to plus and minus twelve), highlights the band via the map's own highlight_utc, captions the selection, and fires on_change with the offset.

Bands are NOMINAL meridians - geometry, not law. Political zone borders and DST belong to whoever supplies offsets to SW_WORLD_CLOCK beside it.

Source: src/sw_timezone_picker.e · since Wave 4 coda - Larry's idea, kept (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Picking a zone

create picker.make
picker.add_marker (class="string">"Denver", 39.7, -105.0)
picker.set_on_change (agent on_zone_picked)
page.put (picker.with_title (class="string">"click any band"))

on_zone_picked (a_offset: INTEGER)
    do
        clock.add_city (class="string">"picked", a_offset * 60)
    end

Known limits

  • Hour bands only (the geometry); half-hour zones are the clock's business.
  • One selection at a time, mirroring the map's single highlight.

Bugs & gotchas

  • offset_at clamps to -12..+12 - the +13/+14 island zones are law, not meridians, and belong to supplied offsets.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Marker-aware picking (click a city, get its REAL offset from a supplied table).

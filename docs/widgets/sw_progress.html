Description

SW_PROGRESS renders a track and an accent fill for a fraction in [0, 1] (invariant-bounded). set_shows_caption adds a right-aligned percentage. It is purely presentational: feed it from your work loop or wire it to other controls.

Source: src/sw_progress.e · since Wave 1 (2026-08-21). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Fed by a button, ten percent at a time

create progress.make (0.0)
card.put (progress)

on_click_me
    do
        clicks := clicks + 1
        progress.set_fraction ((clicks.to_double / 10.0).min (1.0))
    end

Known limits

  • Indeterminate marquee SHIPPED 2026-08-23: set_indeterminate_mode rides the ambient heartbeat.
  • No transition animation between values.

Bugs & gotchas

  • INTEGER / INTEGER division in Eiffel is obsolete-flagged and truncating - convert first (clicks.to_double / 10.0), or the bar jumps in whole steps.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Indeterminate mode (marquee pulse from the tick).
  • Segmented and stacked variants (multi-part totals).
  • Circular progress for dashboards (SW_PAINTER circles are ready).

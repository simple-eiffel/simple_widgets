Description

The 7GUIs Cells engine graduated library-grade as SW_CELLS_ENGINE: '='-led formulas, A0-Z99 references, arithmetic with parentheses, TWO cycle guards (the visiting stack and the structural sweep the value cache taught us to need), and the graduations - RANGES in SUM/AVG/MIN/MAX/COUNT where every member wires a dependency (filling an empty cell inside a summed range propagates - assaulted), TSV blocks in and out (Excel interop for free), CSV of the used extent with honest quoting, and command undo - commit records, undo and redo REPLAY THROUGH COMMIT so propagation is never special-cased.

The aggregate law, stated and assaulted: empty members count for nothing; SUM and COUNT of nothing are 0; AVG, MIN and MAX of nothing are #ERR; any erroring member poisons the whole.

The widget: formula bar naming the anchor, click-select with shift ranges, type-in-place editing (Enter down, Tab right, Escape abandons), Ctrl+C/V as TSV via SW_CLIPBOARD, Ctrl+Z/Y driving the engine's history - all through WM_CHAR control codes, no new key machinery. Deliberately NOT here, per the pinned doctrine: macros, per-cell styling wars, charts-in-cells.

Source: src/sw_spreadsheet.e · since Wave 5 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Cells with truth

create sh.make
sh.engine.commit (sh.engine.key (0, 1), class="string">"10")
sh.engine.commit (sh.engine.key (0, 2), class="string">"=SUM(A0:A2)")
-- the widget renders; the engine answers:
sh.engine.display (sh.engine.key (0, 2))   -- "10"
sh.engine.undo                              -- history walks back

Known limits

  • 26 columns x 100 rows (the Cells benchmark's canvas); no absolute/relative reference distinction.
  • Vertical scroll only; column A..Z fit most widths.

Bugs & gotchas

  • COUNT counts OCCUPIED NUMERIC cells - text cells are nothing to arithmetic.
  • paste_tsv drops cells beyond the sheet's edge rather than wrapping - stated, not silent.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Reference rewriting on row/col insert; larger sheets; fill-down; number formats.

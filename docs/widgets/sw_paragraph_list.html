SW_PARAGRAPH_LIST

Paragraphs of different heights, measured not guessed; a selection set with an anchor; one paragraph editable in place.

Description

The document editor's spine. Where SW_LIST is uniform row height by design, a paragraph is as tall as its own wrap makes it: every paragraph is measured (through the window's shaping kit when the painter carries one - Hebrew, Greek, emoji - or cairo's advances when not), cached by a per-paragraph revision so unchanged text is never re-shaped, and only the paragraphs inside the viewport are painted.

The list knows nothing about its paragraphs beyond their text. What stands in the gutter, what the bands above and below hold, and what colour washes a row are the host's, through agents - a narration studio puts a severity stripe, a voice dot and a fidelity chip there; a notes app puts a date.

Editing in place. begin_edit lays a real SW_TEXT_BOX over the paragraph so its text origin lands exactly where the paragraph was painted. The paragraph follows every keystroke (its height reflows live), the host hears on_edit_change, Escape cancels and restores, and end_edit (True) - or a click on another paragraph - commits through on_edit_commit. The list forwards keys to the editor while it holds focus itself, so typing works the instant the edit begins.

Selection. selected_index is the anchor; is_selected is the set. Plain click selects only, Ctrl toggles, Shift ranges from the anchor. Assigning one property to six paragraphs is one gesture.

Highlights. Every paragraph has its own SW_MARKED_TEXT (marks_of) painted through legend; the in-place editor is handed the same object, so a span follows every keystroke and a cancelled edit restores the marks with the text.

Source: src/sw_paragraph_list.e · since 0.8.0 (2026-09-05), built for simple_narrate's Studio.

A block list with a gutter

create blocks.make (480.0)
blocks.set_gutter_width (36.0)
blocks.set_gutter_renderer (agent draw_stripe_and_dot)
blocks.set_wash_of (agent wash_for_state)
blocks.set_on_activate (agent blocks.begin_edit)
blocks.set_on_edit_commit (agent session.set_block_text)
across session.blocks as b loop blocks.add (b.text) end
blocks.set_band_above (5, 22.0)   -- a plate rule above block 5

Known limits

  • One editor at a time; opening a second edit commits or requires ending the first.
  • Every paragraph is measured for the scrollbar to be honest - fine at hundreds, worth a windowed measure at tens of thousands.
  • Paragraph text is plain; inline emphasis is the host's to draw in a band or the gutter, v1.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Geometry queries (item_top, item_height, item_at) answer only after arrange or draw; is_laid_out says whether they may be asked.
  • A click on another paragraph while editing commits - by design; cancel is Escape.
  • Ctrl and Shift are read from the live keyboard (SW_KEYS); a headless test drives the set through toggle_select and select_range instead.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Drag-to-reorder with a drop line; a split-at-caret command surfacing the editor's offset; a windowed measure for very long documents.

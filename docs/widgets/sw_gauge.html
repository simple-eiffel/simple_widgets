Description

A classic gauge from lower-left around to lower-right. The bands are wedge_fill with close radii - the pie's painter primitive earning double keep as a thick arc. The value band wears its ZONE's semantic colour: accent while calm, warning from warn_from, danger from danger_from - set_zones declares the thresholds, and zone answers 0/1/2 as public, assaulted math beside fraction.

set_value CLAMPS into the span - a gauge shows truth within its range, never a needle wrapped past the pin. A degenerate span (min = max) never sweeps. The demo's gauge is live: it drinks the render bell and swings with every frame's cost, crossing into warning exactly when the heartbeat spikes say it should.

Source: src/sw_gauge.e · since Wave 4, Indicators (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A live gauge off the render bell

create g.make (0.0, 40.0)
g.set_zones (20.0, 33.0)
page.put (g.with_title (class="string">"last frame (live)"))

-- in the bell subscriber:
g.set_value (a_ms.to_double)   -- clamped; zone colour follows

Known limits

  • No needle variant - the band IS the reading (modern instrument style).
  • One value per gauge; multi-band comparisons are a future.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Zones default beyond maximum - a gauge without set_zones never warns.
  • Clamping means an off-scale value reads as PINNED at the end, not as its number - pair with a stat when the raw figure matters.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Tick marks on the track; target markers; multi-band comparison rings.

Description

Not force physics - the classic org drawing: leaves pack left to right by subtree width, every parent centres over its children, rows stack by depth, elbows connect. node_x/node_y/depth_of/subtree_width are deterministic public math; the assault proves the centring law to a ten-thousandth. Click selects and fires on_select.

Source: src/sw_org_chart.e · since Wave 5 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • Top-down only; no collapse yet; wide trees clip rather than shrink.

Bugs & gotchas

  • node_x recurses by subtree - thousands of nodes deserve the future cache, not v1.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Subtree collapse; horizontal orientation; SW_DIAGRAM cross-over view.

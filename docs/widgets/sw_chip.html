Description

SW_CHIP is a compact pill label in one of five kinds - Kind_neutral, Kind_accent, Kind_success, Kind_warning, Kind_danger - each pairing a wash fill with its semantic ink. Use chips to mark state (RENDERED, DIRTY, FAILED), not to decorate: semantic colour is meaning, and the theme's washes keep it legible in both palettes.

Source: src/sw_chip.e · since Wave 1 (2026-08-21). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • Removable chips SHIPPED 2026-08-23: with_remove draws the x, the right zone answers, the agent fires.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Chip text renders at the theme's smallest size (size_chip) - keep labels to a word or two; chips are badges, not sentences.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Removable chips (x zone + removal agent) for tag editors.
  • Choice chips (single/multi select sets) - the web sweep's filter-chip pattern.
  • Counting badges attachable to other widgets (with SW_BADGE in Wave 3).

Description

SW_CALENDAR renders a month: title with prev/next chevrons, a day-of-week header rotated to the effective locale's first day, six weeks of cells. Today wears an accent ring; the selection an accent fill; out-of-month days are muted but pickable (clicking one moves the shown month with it). on_pick (year, month, day) fires on every day click.

All date arithmetic is SIMPLE_DATE's (simple_datetime) - the ecosystem-first rule made the toolkit hand-roll nothing: month stepping wraps years through plus_months/minus_months, and the first-cell computation (first_cell_date) is public and assault-pinned: August 2026 opens on July 26 Sunday-first, July 27 Monday-first.

Source: src/sw_calendar.e · since Wave 3 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Standalone, and inside a picker

create cal.make                      -- shows the current month
cal.set_on_pick (agent on_picked)
page.put (cal)

-- with a culture of its own:
cal.set_locale (create {SW_LOCALE}.make_iso)   -- Monday-first header

-- SW_DATE_PICKER builds one of these fresh per popover open
-- and hands your date back through the same on_pick shape.

Known limits

  • Month view only (no year/decade zoom navigation yet).
  • Single date selection (ranges are a future with SW_DATE_RANGE support in simple_datetime already waiting).
  • No min/max selectable-date constraints yet.

Bugs & gotchas

  • The chevron zones are the outer 30px of the title row - clicks on the title text itself deliberately do nothing.
  • select_date is a silent command (shows and selects, fires nothing); only CLICKS fire on_pick - the commands-are-silent convention.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Year and month zoom (click the title to widen).
  • Range selection riding simple_datetime's SIMPLE_DATE_RANGE.
  • Min/max date constraints with muted-and-refusing cells.
  • Week-number rail option.

Description

Labelled slices swept clockwise from twelve o'clock. make_donut gives a hole at 55% of the radius - a TRUE ring: SW_PAINTER grew wedge_fill/wedge_stroke (arc out, arc_negative back, close, fill), no surface-coloured overlay fakery. The palette runs eight distinguishable steps: the four theme semantics, then their washes.

A side legend lists every slice with its colour dot and SHARE (percent of the whole); hovering a slice outlines its wedge, brightens its legend row, and a chip restates label, value and share. The math is public and assaulted headless: slice_at (radius band + clockwise angle walk, with the atan2 quadrant dance written out), percent_of (shares sum to 100), angle_of (normalized 0..2pi from twelve, y-down).

An empty pie says no data yet instead of pretending.

Source: src/sw_pie_chart.e · since Wave 4, Proportions (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A donut of the library

create pie.make_donut
pie.add_slice (class="string">"Wave 1", 24.0)
pie.add_slice (class="string">"Wave 2", 12.0)
pie.add_slice (class="string">"Wave 3", 25.0)
page.put (pie.with_title (class="string">"the library as a donut"))

-- headless truth:
pie.percent_of (2)          -- 19.7
pie.slice_at (px, py)       -- which wedge is under a point

Known limits

  • Eight palette steps; a ninth slice reuses colours - group small slices first.
  • No exploded slices or per-slice offsets.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Slices sweep in ADD ORDER, clockwise from twelve - order is meaning.
  • The donut hole answers slice_at with 0 - it is honestly nobody.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • An 'other' auto-grouping for long tails; exploded emphasis; click-to-select.

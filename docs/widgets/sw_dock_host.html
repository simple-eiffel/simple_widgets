SW_DOCK_HOST + SW_DOCK_ZONE

TRUE DOCKING: reflow panels in west/east/south zones around a centre document; empty zones collapse to nothing.

Description

The promise kept from the day the overlap law was declared: workbench panels that RESHAPE the page instead of floating over it. Three zones, each a real widget stacking its panels under title bars; the centre document takes every pixel the zones do not; an EMPTY ZONE COLLAPSES TO ZERO - zone_rect and center_rect are public reflow math, assaulted (occupied west takes its fraction, empty east is nothing, the centre grows the moment a zone empties).

Panels move by pick-and-drop - middle-click a title bar, drop on another zone - or programmatically via move_panel; both fire on_layout_change. Hit testing routes THROUGH to panel contents, so docked widgets stay fully alive.

Source: src/sw_dock_host.e · since Wave 5 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A workbench

create dh.make (document)
p1 := dh.add_panel (class="string">"explorer", tree, dh.Zone_west)
p2 := dh.add_panel (class="string">"output", log, dh.Zone_south)
dh.move_panel (p1, dh.Zone_east)   -- west collapses, east stands up

Known limits

  • Three zones (west/east/south) v1; zone fractions are settable but not grip-draggable yet.
  • No floating windows - docked or nothing, by design.

Bugs & gotchas

  • An empty zone is INVISIBLE - that is the reflow promise, not a missing panel.
  • Panel ids are stable; zones change, ids never do (the kanban's law).

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Zone-edge resize grips; drop-hint overlays while a pebble flies; panel close/collapse buttons; layout persistence.

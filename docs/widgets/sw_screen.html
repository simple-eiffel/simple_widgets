Description

SW_SCREEN marries SHELL_DESKTOP's raw desktop grab (simple_shell) to cairo: grab (x, y, w, h) BitBlts any virtual-screen region into a fresh ARGB32 CAIRO_SURFACE - ready for write_png, an SW_IMAGE, or any painter. It is the pure-route EV_SCREEN, completed: no Vision2, no separate capture library. Virtual-screen metrics (virtual_x/y/width/height), the QPC clock (now_ms) and open_externally ride along from the platform shell.

Source: src/sw_screen.e · born in THE CARVE (2026-08-23), the dividend of moving all Win32 C into simple_shell. Assaulted every suite run: six real desktop pixels, grabbed and sized.

Grab and keep

local
    screen: SW_SCREEN
do
    create screen
    if attached screen.grab (screen.virtual_x, screen.virtual_y, 800, 600) as shot then
        shot.write_png ("capture.png").do_nothing
        shot.destroy
    end
end

The grab answers Void - never crashes - when the desktop cannot be read (locked session, secure screen).

Known limits

  • Alpha is forced opaque by design: the desktop has no meaningful alpha, and downstream cairo compositing expects solid ground.
  • One region per call; a multi-monitor mosaic is one grab spanning the virtual desktop (negative origins included), not per-monitor calls.
  • Protected content (DRM surfaces) arrives black - the OS's law, not ours.

Bugs & gotchas

  • The returned surface is yours: destroy it when done, as with any CAIRO_SURFACE you create.
  • Coordinates are virtual-desktop absolute - a monitor left of primary makes virtual_x negative; pass metrics through, never assume 0.

Future

  • A region-picker flow over SHELL_OVERLAY (the frozen-desktop drag), returning a grab of the chosen rectangle - simple_ocr_capture's pattern, generalized.
  • Timed capture series for screen-recording experiments.

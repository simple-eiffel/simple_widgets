Description

Nodes are assigned to columns (add_node returns the id; add_link requires rightward flow by contract). A node's throughput is the larger of its inflow and outflow - that drives its bar height, proportional within each column. Every link becomes a cubic-bezier ribbon: the painter gained ribbon_fill (two curved rails, midpoint control) exactly where the roadmap said curves would arrive. Ribbons moor at STACKED offsets down each bar - the assault proves the moorings are contiguous and fill the source bar exactly.

Ribbons wash in their source's colour at 35% alpha, bars draw over them, labels sit left of the first column and right of the rest. Hovering a bar names the node with its in and out totals - imbalance is visible truth, not an error. The layout is public math: refresh_layout, node_rects, link_bands, throughput_of, all assaulted headless.

Source: src/sw_sankey.e · since Wave 4, Flows (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A pipeline as ribbons

create sk.make
n1 := sk.add_node (class="string">"captured", 1)
n2 := sk.add_node (class="string">"OCR clean", 2)
n3 := sk.add_node (class="string">"OCR fail", 2)
n4 := sk.add_node (class="string">"exported", 3)
sk.add_link (n1, n2, 870.0)
sk.add_link (n1, n3, 130.0)
sk.add_link (n2, n4, 640.0)
page.put (sk.with_title (class="string">"the pipeline as ribbons"))

Known limits

  • Ribbon hit-testing is a stated future - hover answers on NODE bars today.
  • Link order is add order; no crossing minimization yet.
  • Columns are explicit - no automatic layering from the link graph.

Bugs & gotchas

  • add_link's contract demands source.column < target.column - back-edges and same-column links are refused, not drawn badly.
  • A node whose flows are imbalanced shows it: ribbons cover only part of the taller side's bar. That is the diagram telling the truth.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Ribbon hover (nearest-band math); crossing reduction; automatic column layering.
  • value labels on thick ribbons.

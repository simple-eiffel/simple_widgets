SW_CROP_BOX

A drag marquee over an image reporting NORMALIZED crop fractions - any drag direction.

Description

Press, drag, release: on_crop fires (fx, fy, fw, fh) as 0..1 fractions of the face, corners sorted whichever way the drag ran, wild drags clamped into the unit box - all assaulted. Mapping through the image's own letterboxing is the stated next step.

Source: src/sw_crop_box.e · since Wave 6 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Cropping

create cb.make (class="string">"photo.png")
cb.set_on_crop (agent on_crop_changed)

Known limits

  • Fractions are of the WIDGET face, not yet of the image's letterboxed pixels.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Every press starts a NEW marquee - there are no resize handles on the old one yet.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Letterbox-aware image-space fractions; corner handles; fixed-ratio locks.

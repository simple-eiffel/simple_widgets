Description

A fixed grid of values washed along a channel-wise blend from the theme's quiet surface to its accent. The blend math is public and assaulted at its endpoints (0 keeps the from-colour, 1 lands the to-colour, 0.5 splits every channel). heat_of normalizes each cell against the grid's own low and high - and a FLAT grid washes everything to the honest midpoint instead of dividing by nothing.

Row labels ride the left edge, column labels the bottom. Hover outlines the cell and a chip names row / column / value. The slot arithmetic (row_at, col_at) is the bar chart's idiom, twice, and equally assaulted. Cells keep a one-pixel breathing gap - the banding discipline SW_SHEET taught.

Source: src/sw_heatmap.e · since Wave 4, Densities (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

An activity grid

create hm.make (5, 12)
hm.set_row_labels (<<class="string">"Mon", class="string">"Tue", class="string">"Wed", class="string">"Thu", class="string">"Fri">>)
hm.set_cell (2, 7, 14.0)
page.put (hm.with_title (class="string">"activity"))

hm.heat_of (2, 7)     -- 0..1 against the grid's own span
hm.blend (a, b, 0.5)  -- the ramp math, public

Known limits

  • Two-stop ramp (surface to accent); multi-stop palettes are future.
  • The grid is fixed at creation - no add_row/add_col growth.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Normalization is grid-relative: one outlier cools everything else. Clamp your data first when outliers lie.
  • Column labels crowd under narrow cells - label sparsely (every Nth) on wide grids.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Multi-stop ramps and diverging palettes; per-cell annotations; column label thinning.

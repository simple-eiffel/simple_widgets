Description

Seven day columns named by the theme's locale, a minute axis through SW_SCALE, and the lane law: greedy lowest-free-lane in add order - assaulted on the classic triple (A, overlapping B, back-to-back C reclaiming lane one).

Source: src/sw_scheduler.e · since Wave 5 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

A week

create sc.make
sc.add_event (2, 9 * 60, 10 * 60, class="string">"standup")
sc.add_event (2, 9 * 60 + 30, 11 * 60, class="string">"review")  -- lane two
sc.set_window (7 * 60, 19 * 60)

Known limits

  • Single week, no dates - pair with SW_CALENDAR for navigation.
  • No drag-to-move events yet.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Lanes are per-DAY: the same meeting pattern lanes independently each day.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Event drag; day/agenda variants; calendar pairing.

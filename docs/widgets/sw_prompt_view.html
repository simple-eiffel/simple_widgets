Description

Submit posts a mine-bubble, clears the box, fires on_submit; the answering side streams through begin_reply / append_token / end_reply into one growing theirs-bubble. say_system narrates. The demo wires an echo engine; simple_narrate's plumbing is the real mate, as the roadmap pinned. Round trip and streaming assaulted.

Source: src/sw_prompt_view.e · since Wave 6 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Wired to an engine

create pv.make
pv.set_on_submit (agent ask_engine)
-- engine callbacks:
pv.begin_reply
pv.append_token (chunk)
pv.end_reply

Known limits

  • Single-line prompt box v1 (multiline follows the text box's own growth).

Bugs & gotchas

  • An empty box submits nothing - by contract of use, not by error.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Multiline prompts; stop-generation button; simple_narrate demo marriage.

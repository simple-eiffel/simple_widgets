Description

SW_SPACER is an invisible widget whose only property is grow = 1 at birth: it absorbs a container's slack. Its defining trick is the pinned footer (see SW_COLUMN); in rows it pushes siblings apart (left-cluster / right-cluster toolbars).

Source: src/sw_spacer.e · since Wave 2 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • It has no minimum: with zero slack it vanishes entirely - BY DESIGN (verdict 2026-08-23): vanishing IS the spacer's contract; a spacer that refuses to vanish is a margin, and margins belong to pads.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Two spacers share slack equally (both grow 1.0) - centering trick: spacer, content, spacer.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Weighted convenience creation (make_weighted (2.0)) - today use set_grow after creation.

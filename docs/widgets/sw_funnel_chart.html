Description

Stages narrow with their values, centred symmetrically; each band joins the next by its slanted sides (polygon_fill earns its keep) and is washed by its conversion - stronger alpha for stronger stages. Every band writes its label, value, and conversion from the first stage - the number funnels exist to show. Hover solidifies a band and bolds its line.

The contract enforces the shape: add_stage requires descending values - a funnel that widens is a lie the compiler now refuses. stage_at and conversion_of are public math, assaulted headless.

Source: src/sw_funnel_chart.e · since Wave 4, Proportions (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • Strictly descending stages by contract; equal values are allowed, growth is not.
  • Single funnel - no side-by-side cohort comparison yet.

Bugs & gotchas

  • Labels sit ON the bands; very narrow tails can crowd - keep stage names short.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Cohort comparison (two funnels sharing a scale); stage-to-stage drop annotations.

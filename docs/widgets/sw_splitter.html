Description

SW_SPLITTER arranges a left and right child around a draggable divider. The ratio is invariant-clamped to [0.15, 0.85]: no drag can crush a pane to nothing. The divider wears a hover affordance (an accent-washed grip bar) so users know it moves before they try.

Source: src/sw_splitter.e · since Wave 1 (2026-08-21). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • Two panes, vertical divider only (no horizontal split or three-pane nesting sugar - nest splitters for now).
  • No collapse-to-edge gesture.
  • Divider cursor SHIPPED 2026-08-23: east-west resize arrows via cursor_kind.

Bugs & gotchas

  • The clamp is a real contract: set_ratio (0.05) violates a precondition under assertions rather than silently pinning - clamp in the caller if your input is user-generated.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Horizontal orientation; double-click the divider to reset the ratio.
  • Collapse buttons on the grip; remembered ratios (app settings integration).

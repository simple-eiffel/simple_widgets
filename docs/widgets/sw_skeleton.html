Description

SW_SKELETON draws rounded placeholder lines (the last one short, like real text) with a soft shimmer band that drifts across on every render. The window's heartbeat repaints twice a second, so waiting screens visibly breathe even when nothing else moves - SW_SKELETON is the widget that made the heartbeat's repaint unconditional.

Source: src/sw_skeleton.e · since Wave 3 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Known limits

  • Line shapes only - no circle/rect skeleton variants (avatar/image placeholders) yet.
  • Shimmer advances per render (~2fps on the heartbeat): deliberate calm, not a spinner.

Bugs & gotchas

  • The shimmer phase advances in draw - a busy window (mouse movement) shimmers faster than an idle one. Cosmetic, and honest in its own way.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Shape variants (disc, rect, card composite) mirroring the content they stand for.
  • A faster dedicated animation tick if the heartbeat gains variable rate.

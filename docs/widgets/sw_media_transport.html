Description

The control surface of playback with none of the codec: hosts subscribe on_play/on_pause/on_seek and report progress through set_position. simple_audio's player is the natural mate. format_clock refuses to lie on the hour boundary (75:00, never a wrap); seek math clamps; all assaulted.

Source: src/sw_media_transport.e · since Wave 6 (2026-08-22). Live in the showroom: demo/sw_demo.e.

Wired to a host

create tr.make (245.0)
tr.set_on_seek (agent player_seek)
tr.set_on_play (agent player_go)
-- heartbeat: tr.set_position (player_position)

Known limits

  • No volume or scrubbing preview; the bar is position only, v1.

Bugs & gotchas

  • set_position clamps into the duration - a runaway host reads as pinned at the end, not as an error.

Extension plans & possibilities

  • Volume flyout; buffered-range shading; simple_audio player marriage in the demo.
